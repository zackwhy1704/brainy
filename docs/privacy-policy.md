# Second Brain — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-01.

Second Brain stores what you share into it and answers questions about it later. This policy
describes exactly what the app does with your data.

## What is collected

Only what you deliberately share into the app: text, links, screenshots/images, and PDFs, plus
the questions you type into Ask. The app creates an anonymous identifier for your installation;
no name, email, or password is collected.

## What happens to it

- Shared content and data derived from it (summaries, topics, facts about people mentioned) are
  stored on our backend, hosted by Supabase, under your anonymous identifier.
- To produce summaries, extract facts, and answer questions, your shared content and questions
  are sent to two AI service providers acting on our behalf: **Anthropic** (Claude API) and
  **OpenAI** (embeddings API). Under their commercial API terms, this data is not used to train
  their models.
- All transfers use TLS encryption.

## What is not done

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no sale of data, no sharing with anyone beyond the service
providers named above. Content you share is never visible to other users.

## Deletion and retention

- Deleting an item in the app deletes it and everything derived from it (summary, extracted
  facts, search vectors, uploaded files) from the backend.
- The anonymous identifier lives only on your device. If you uninstall the app, the identifier
  is destroyed and your stored data becomes permanently inaccessible — to you and to anyone
  else. Residual inaccessible records may persist on the backend until purged.
- For data questions or deletion requests, contact: zhengyi1704@gmail.com.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with a new effective date.
